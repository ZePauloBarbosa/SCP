    Mock notes (not part of the UI)

    • Address → Customer. The reverse-geocoded address is replaced by the matched customer (name + service address).
    • Candidates = customers on the truck's route whose pickup location is within 40 yd of the inference point. The dashed circle shows that area: 40 yd radius, 80 yd across.
    • Placeholder color rules (to be defined with Ryan / data team):
      Green — one candidate. Auto-assigned, locked.
      Yellow — 2+ candidates, closest is ≤ half the distance of the next. Closest pre-selected.
      Red — 2+ candidates, no clear leader. Nothing selected.
    • Hover a row → its marker highlights on the map. Click outside the map and table (search, page margins, title) → zooms back to the full view.
    • One table. Options = images only. Status column = Open/Cleared with × (clear) or undo (reopen). "Confirm" is a text link under the dropdown, right-aligned — the same spot and style as "Change" on confirmed rows. Picking a customer (table dropdown, panel list or map letter) only selects it; ✓ or "Confirm & next" confirms. Confirmed rows show a check before the name and a Change link; the ✓ spot is left blank. Picking a different customer on the map for a confirmed or auto-matched row un-confirms it until you confirm again. Undo appears only on cleared rows (reopen).
    • Panel: "Confirm & next" confirms the selected customer, or a Status change (Open ⇄ Cleared), and moves to the next row. A Status change in the panel isn't applied until confirmed; leaving the insight discards it. Plain "Next" when there's nothing to confirm.
    • Cleared rows show "No customer selected" and "Cleared · who · date".
    • Click any row → map zooms to the inference, highlights nearby properties (A–D) with distances. Click a letter on the map or in the card to pick that customer.
    • Right panel (Edit Street pattern): no Save — picking a customer or changing Status applies immediately. Previous / Next (or ← →) steps through the table rows. Esc or X closes.
    • X in the row clears the insight. A cleared row shows an undo icon in the same spot to reopen it, and its customer can't be changed until it's reopened.
    • Image viewer is a carousel: hopper-camera image first, then street-view images of nearby properties (arrows or ← → keys).
    • Card keeps today's popup fields (Date, Customer, Vehicle, Status, image). Click the image for the full-size viewer.
    • Yellow / red / purple are not in the SCP design system — need design tokens.
    • Map is a drawn stand-in, not real map tiles.
